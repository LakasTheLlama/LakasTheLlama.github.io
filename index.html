Who we are

Central Coast Volleyball Club Inc (CCVC) is located between Sydney and Newcastle in NSW. We are a Volleyball NSW-affiliated & not-for-profit club and cater to all levels of ability, from new starters through to experienced and the more competitive players. ​If you are looking to play at a competitive level, or simply want a more social and fun way to keep fit, please come and see us. CCVC aims to provide opportunities for all people to enjoy sport, make new friends, learn new skills, acquire habits of self-discipline, learn to cooperate and to compete fairly.
