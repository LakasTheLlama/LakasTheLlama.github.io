Who We Are

Central Coast Volleyball Club Inc (CCVC) is located between Sydney and Newcastle in NSW. We are a Volleyball NSW-affiliated, not-for-profit club catering to all levels of ability.

Whether you're looking to play at a competitive level or simply want a fun way to keep fit and make new friends, come see us. CCVC provides opportunities for everyone to enjoy sport, learn new skills, and compete fairly.
