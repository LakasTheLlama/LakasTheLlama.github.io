Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about CCVC, our sessions, registration, and more.

Registration is done through Volleyball NSW's online system. Head to our Register page for a direct link and step-by-step instructions. You'll need to create a Volleyball NSW account and select CCVC as your club.

Once registered you'll receive a confirmation email. Bring it (or show it on your phone) the first time you attend a session.

Membership fees vary depending on the type of membership (social, competitive, junior, etc.). Current pricing is listed on the Register page.

There may also be a small Volleyball NSW affiliation fee in addition to the club membership fee. Both are payable online at time of registration.

Absolutely! We run a dedicated Beginners Training session every Thursday evening at Niagara Park Stadium. Our coaches start from the very basics – no experience required.

Just make sure you're registered before stepping on court. See the Register page to get started.

We run a wide range of sessions across the week:

Monday: Women's & Girls Reps, Men's & Boys Reps (Niagara Park Stadium)
Thursday: Beginners Training (5:30 PM) + ABC Grade Training (7:00 PM)
Friday: Scrimmage Games
Sunday: Social Competition (Terrigal/GYG Stadium)
Saturday: SVL Men's Div 2 games at Sydney Olympic Park (in-season)

Visit the Play page for the full interactive timetable.

For indoor sessions: court shoes (non-marking soles), comfortable athletic wear, a water bottle, and your registration confirmation for your first session.

Knee pads are recommended for defensive players but not mandatory. Balls are provided by the club.

Our social competition runs on Sunday evenings. Teams are allocated courts and matched up against other CCVC teams of a similar level. Each week's draw is published on the home page schedule – including which team is on duty (setting up and packing away).

Social comp is open to all registered members. If you don't have a team, let us know and we'll try to place you with one.

Our rep program (Women's/Men's & Girls/Boys) competes in state-level competitions through Volleyball NSW. Selection is managed by our coaches and typically takes place at the start of each season.

Attend regular training sessions and express your interest to a coach or the committee. Contact us for more information.

Club merch (hoodies, water bottles, jerseys etc.) is ordered in bulk once enough members have submitted requests. Head to the Merch page, select your item and size, and submit a request.

We'll contact you with updates once an order is ready to go out, and sort payment at that time. This means there can sometimes be a wait before items arrive.

Yes! We welcome junior players and have a Youth Policy in place to ensure a safe and positive environment. All our coaches have valid Working With Children clearances.

Junior members must be registered through Volleyball NSW, and parent/guardian consent is required. See our Youth Policy for full details.

The best ways to reach us are via the Contact page form, email at secretary.ccvc@gmail.com, or through our Instagram and Facebook pages.

We aim to respond within 2 business days. For urgent matters use social media DMs.
